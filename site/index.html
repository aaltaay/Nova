What it actually does

Built for one operator and one Gateway.

01

IBKR scanner rosters

Gainers, Gappers, Afterhours, and Large Cap from persistent IBKR subscriptions. Empty is not fresh. Frozen tables stay frozen after the session boundary.

02

HOD Momo

Session-high alerts from the names already on the desk. Tick-driven. No extra historical budget stolen from charts.

03

Trader View

Tabs for the names you are actually trading. Level 2 and tape live here, not in the scanner sidebar. Cap matches the IBKR depth plan.

04

Store-first charts

Archived minutes paint immediately. Background fills catch up. A click never waits behind unrelated cold work on the shared socket.

05

Gated paper execution

Orders go through one command path. You arm paper. Live needs a second confirm. Unattended live auto-trading is rejected in code.

06

Alerts off the glass

Discord and Telegram when a setup fires, so you can look up from the chair without staring at every tick.
