# ADR 046 -- The Sim replays the operator's Massive flat files: the whole tape and the bid and ask

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-06
**Amends:** [[017-single-replay-surface]] (a historical window has a second source, still one replay surface) · [[019-practice-fills-on-replayed-sessions]] (a Massive window fills at the bid and ask) · [[012-local-first-chart-bars]] (Massive bars never enter the IBKR chart store)
**Decided by:** the operator, 2026-10-06:
- "u wanna populate the data inside the sim? that way when we go back in time, the data we downloaded all of its information gets picked up?"
- On the plan (the tape and candles first, then the bid and ask, then the boards): "1 go also do the bid and ask."

## Context

A Sim historical window came from one place: an IBKR download. IBKR pages historical trades at 1,000 prints a request, one request every 11 s -- about 90 prints a second -- so a full day of AAPL on 2026-10-02 (628,348 trades) would take about two hours and is over the 500,000 prints a selection holds. The download carries no bid or ask: practice orders price from prints alone, and a print has a side only where the local Level 2 recorder happened to run.

The operator now keeps Massive Stocks Advanced flat files under `E:\Nova\massive` (`trades_v1`, `quotes_v1`, `minute_aggs_v1`, one gzip CSV per dataset per trading day, every US stock, sorted by ticker), downloading newest year first back to 2016. One day (2026-10-02): trades 3.12 GB = 129,362,217 rows; quotes 9.62 GB = 537,897,040 rows. The quotes are the NBBO -- the best bid and best ask across all venues, a row at every change -- not depth. Nothing in Nova read them: the Sim never picked up what was downloaded.

## Decision

1. **A second source for a historical window, on the same surface.** A window is an IBKR download or an import from the Massive files (`source: "massive"`). Selection, the playhead, coverage, the charts, Time & Sales, Level 2 and practice fills read both the same way; the snapshot says which.
2. **`auto` picks the source.** `POST /api/sim/history` and `POST /api/sim/history/select` take `source: "auto" | "ibkr" | "massive"` (default `auto`). A download takes the Massive files when that day's trades file is whole on disk, else IBKR. A selection keeps what exists (a Massive import of the window, else an IBKR download of it), else takes the Massive files when they are on disk. **Selecting a day that is on disk starts its import**, so going back in time picks the data up: the window loads empty, and the desk's quiet re-select folds it in when the import completes.
3. **The import reads, it does not change.** For one ticker it streams each day file once, to the end of that ticker's block (there is no index; a ticker late in the alphabet costs most of the file: measured, TSLA 09:30-09:32 read in 232 s, AAPL in 8 s). Rows keep Massive's values -- price, size, nanosecond SIP time, exchange, conditions, correction; the only conversions are nanoseconds to epoch seconds and an exchange id to its name. Every field is parsed with the csv module (conditions hold quoted commas). The window's trades, 1-minute bars and NBBO rows are written in one transaction, so a window is whole or absent; the quote standing at the window's open is carried in with it.
4. **Which prints set a price.** Every print is shown. A print moves the last, a candle, the volume or a practice fill only when its conditions are not the SIP's volume-only set (2, 7, 10, 13, 15, 16, 20, 21, 22, 29, 37, 38, 52, 53 -- the conditions IBKR's letters name in `TAPE_NO_PRICE_CONDITIONS`) and its correction is 0 or 12. A trade later corrected, marked erroneous or cancelled (1, 7, 8) shows and sets nothing; cancel and error records (10, 11) are not trades and are dropped. Checked on AAPL 2026-10-02 09:30-09:40: candles built from the prints that set a price equal Massive's own minute bars in open, high, low and close for all 10 minutes; their volume is lower (odd lots are excluded, as the live desk counts IBKR's tape).
5. **The bid and ask, never read ahead.** The snapshot's `bid` / `ask` (with sizes and venues) are the last NBBO row at or before the playhead. A print's side is judged against the last NBBO row strictly before it, by the live tape's rule (`ibkr/tape_side`), so a crossed quote or none decides nothing (`side_source: "nbbo"`). With no recorded book for the moment, Level 2 shows the NBBO as one level flagged `l1_fallback` -- best bid and ask only, never depth, which the files do not carry. Practice orders at placement fill at the far side (`fill_basis: "quote"`); resting orders still fill on later prints that set a price.
6. **A day whose quotes are not on disk yet** imports its trades and bars and says so (`quote_status: "not_downloaded"`); loading the window again after the quotes file arrives imports it again with its bid and ask, while the window imported before keeps playing (the store replaces it in one transaction), and the desk folds the bid and ask in when that import completes.
7. **Out of the API process.** The import runs as its own process (`python -m sim.massive_worker`, or `nova-api.exe --massive-import` frozen), below normal priority, one at a time, beside an IBKR download: inflating gigabytes and parsing a ticker's rows never shares the GIL with the loops that carry the desk's Level 2, tape and orders (ADR 045). The three files are read side by side. Pause ends the process; nothing is written before the final transaction.
8. **No mixing.** Massive bars and prints never enter the IBKR chart store (`bars_store`), and a Massive window's candles come only from its own prints and 1-minute bars. The single-market-data-feed rule's Sim exception names this source.
9. **On the Massive drive.** `NOVA_MARKET_DATA_DIR` (the research scripts' variable) names the files' root, default `E:\Nova\massive`; the imported windows live beside them in `<root>/sim/replay.sqlite3`, their own schema, apart from the IBKR download store on F:. A window over 500,000 prints or 4,000,000 quotes is refused with the count before anything is written. A store that cannot be read is said in the listing (`massive.store_error`), never listed as no imports.
10. **Going back in time picks it up on the desk.** The Sim Day calendar marks every day whose trades are in the files and lets it be opened (until now a day needed a Scanner board or a Session Record, so nothing before September 2021 could be). A Sim tab on such a day offers **Load from files** -- no Gateway, no IBKR pacing -- follows the import and loads the window when it finishes. The quote card and the ticket take the bid and ask at the playhead, Time & Sales colours prints from the NBBO and dims those that set no price, and Level 2 says it shows the NBBO only. The Historical replay panel says which days the files hold and lists imports beside downloads, each file's share read while it runs.

## How it was checked

- 26 backend tests on small files in Massive's own format (quoted commas, a ticker's block across every chunk size, the last line with no newline, `.part` files, every price rule, the window and its opening quote, the caps, pause, a crashed process, the never-ahead rules, practice fills at the far side, the IBKR chart store never read, re-import with the window still playing, an unreadable store stated).
- Real files, AAPL 2026-10-02: the bid/ask at 09:30:00.0035 matched an independent read of the quotes file (333.19 x 120 / 333.33 x 40); trade-built candles matched Massive's minute bars 10 of 10.
- The desk: 4,214 unit tests, among them the offer on a Massive day with the Gateway down, the import's own slot, the re-select rules, the NBBO ladder note and the calendar's fourth fact.

## Consequences

- Any symbol-day whose files are down replays with its full tape and the real bid and ask, in seconds to a few minutes instead of hours, and practice fills become spread-aware.
- The first open of a ticker late in the alphabet waits for most of a 3 GB and a 9.6 GB file to be read; the desk shows the import's progress. A second open is instant.
- The Massive store grows with each window imported; deleting it only means importing again.
- Not built here: the scanner boards for those days (the leaderboard rebuild already reads the minute files), and a previous close from the Massive files (the replay's `prev_close` keeps #542's order and stays a stated absence when none answers).

## Amendment -- the files first, and the rest of the Sim reads them (2026-10-09)

**Decided by:** the operator, 2026-10-09, on a Sim tab of WFF that offered only an IBKR download: "why massive data isn't being loaded here? sholdn't we have prioritized it over ibkr data?", then "1 go yes + however i dont think the entire nova app recorgnize massive as data recap for the sim in the first place. please figure it out".

**What was found.** That day (2026-10-09) was not in the files yet: Massive publishes a day once it has ended, and the downloader picks it up the next morning (2026-10-08's trades file landed 03:37 ET on the 9th). But the audit behind the second ask found the Sim used the files only for one ticker's window, loaded on a click: an IBKR download of the same hours won over them; the charts drew only the window's bars and nothing before it; Gap% fell back to the IBKR chart store; the prior close never read them, so a day no board or download covered had no change, no Gap% and no SSR reference.

**Decided.**
1. **The files win under `auto`.** A selection takes a Massive import holding the window, else the day's files on disk, even over an IBKR download of the same hours. The download plays only when the files' import of the window failed, or the day is not in them. Supersedes decision 2's "keeps what exists".
2. **The day around the window.** An import keeps the ticker's whole day of 1-minute bars (at most 960 rows); the charts draw the day before the window from them, never past the window's end or the playhead. A window imported before is imported again on its next load, and keeps playing meanwhile. The session open comes from the window's own 09:30 bar, never the IBKR chart store (decision 8).
3. **The prior close from the day bars.** `day_aggs_v1`'s close is the official close (GRML 2026-09-18 2.85 and WHLR 2026-09-22 1.87, both IBKR's tick 9). It answers after the recording, the leaderboard and an IBKR download -- it is not split-adjusted -- and only the prior session's own file answers. Supersedes the consequence that left "a previous close from the Massive files" unbuilt.
4. **It loads by itself on an empty desk.** The Trader tab on screen, on a day in the files, with nothing loaded, imports and loads its window without a click, as the agent's `show` already did; never over a loaded replay, never an IBKR download, once per window. A day newer than the files says it is not out yet, and when the newest day landed.

**Still not built** (tracked in one issue): the scanner boards, HOD Momo and the movers index rebuilt from the files as a day lands (built by hand today, or empty); the Full Day chart, daily levels and Rel Vol from the day bars (they read IBKR daily bars, about five years deep); the socket-fed engines -- LULD, the sensors, the `/api/ticker` snapshot -- on any historical window; Volume boost and Contenders on a past day; the replay's `replay_key` naming its source.
