# ADR 050 -- Agents find stock-days and show them in the Sim

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-06
**Amends:** [[046-sim-replays-the-massive-flat-files]] (a Massive day can now be found, not only opened) · [[033-focus-and-book-watch-sensors]] (agents read the desk through one more door; the focus sensor is unchanged)
**Decided by:** the operator, 2026-10-06:
- The ask: "if I speak to you (for example, 'Go to a small-cap stock that increased, where the high of the day was 300% or something like that'), I can navigate through the UI and punch it in on the simulator so I could see it".
- On where: "this is just going to be for agents for now. Literally, here as I'm speaking to you, when I ask such things, I want the agents to be able to find it and help navigate it, like some secret endpoints that they can manipulate and show me." On voice: "I'm just going to type my requests here ... as simple as possible as of this moment."
- On words: "If I'm obviously asking for a command, such as this one, I want it to be added to our dictionary." On shapes: "you could ask the user, 'What does it mean?'", "most of it should be in the bot strategies ... I don't want you to invent stuff ... If you give me a shape, then I may tell you to fine-tune it."
- On the desk: an agent may switch it to the Sim "when nothing is at stake". On float: "proven, else unknown".

## Context

The operator practises on past days in the Sim (ADR 046: any day whose Massive trades file is on disk replays with its whole tape and the NBBO). Finding the day was the hard part: the Scanner boards exist for 75 days (ADR 023), and nothing could answer "which small caps ran 300%?" across the 2,515 sessions of Massive candles on `E:\Nova\massive` (Oct 2016 on). Counted on the day bars: 6,104 stock-days had a regular-hours high at least +300% over the prior close; 5,517 of them were warrants, rights, units, names under 50 cents or likely reverse splits, leaving 587 common-stock days, 522 of them replayable. Massive's day bar is regular hours only (checked: AAPL and GME on 2026-10-05, and 2,477 of 11,866 tickers on 2026-09-09 had a premarket or after-hours high above it), so a premarket run is invisible there.

Getting a day on screen took five clicks in a fixed order (venue, Sim day, a Trader tab, Load from files, the playhead), and nothing outside the desk window could make them: there was no channel from the backend to the desk for anything but market data.

## Decision

1. **For agents, not the desk UI.** The operator types to an agent (Claude here, or Codex or Cursor); the agent understands the request, asks when a word has no agreed meaning, and calls private endpoints under `/api/agent`. They answer only on this PC, every write needs the desk's API key even on loopback, and no desk screen shows them. Nova holds no speech-to-text and no model for this: the agent is the understanding. A small CLI (`tools/nova_agent.py`) and a skill (`.claude/skills/nova-sim-navigator`) let any agent session find and use them without seeing the key.
2. **The day movers index.** One row per stock per session that moved, built from the Massive day and minute bars by `research/movers/build_movers.py` and written through `backend/day_movers/store.py` into `<NOVA_MARKET_DATA_DIR>/movers/day_movers.sqlite3` (ADR 024's rule: the backend reads only its own store). Definitions:
   - the prior close is the official close of the immediately prior session in the files, multiplied through any split listed (Massive's list, or one `confirm_splits.py` proved) executing in between; none when the stock did not trade that session -- never an older close;
   - the day's high and low count 04:00-20:00 from the minute bars, beside the regular-hours open, high, low and official close of the day bar;
   - the type is the Massive reference's (today's dump, delisted tickers included); a ticker reused by another company reads today's type;
   - an overnight open 1.8x or more (0.7x or less) the prior close with no listed split is a **split suspect**, and a **likely split** when it traded no more shares than the session before (a reverse split shrinks the share count; 30 of the 31 real 3x jumps in #772 traded 10-1,000x more). Suspects are tagged, never dropped.
   A row is kept when the stock moved: the high +10% or the low -10% from the prior close, a close or a gap of 5% either way, or a split suspect. It builds newest day first, in the background, and is extended by running the builder again.
3. **Numbers in, numbers out; the words live in a dictionary.** The search takes plain numbers (percent moves of the high, low, close and gap against the prior close, where the close sits in the day's range, how much of a run was given back, when the high printed, price, volume, float) and answers with the matches and their percentages. Nova bakes in no meaning for "ascending", "fader" or "small cap". The agent turns a word into numbers, shows them with the result, and the operator tunes them. A phrasing the operator confirms becomes a **dictionary** entry -- the words, what they mean, the exact call -- kept by the backend as operator data, so the next agent reads the operator the same way. Shapes the bot already knows come from the bot strategies' own detectors, never from an agent's invention (the strategy finder is the next step on this door).
4. **The desk follows.** `POST /api/agent/show` and `POST /api/agent/move` queue a command; the main desk window takes it by a long poll and runs the same steps a person clicks -- venue, Sim day, Trader tab, the Massive window, the wait for its import, the playhead -- reporting each step back, with a notice on the desk saying what it is doing. Only the main window takes commands; the sample desk and the demo build never do. A busy runner's window that is over the Sim's print cap is retried narrower. Nothing on this path places, stages or cancels an order, arms the desk, or touches Live trading.
5. **The venue moves only when nothing is at stake.** When the desk is not on the Sim, `show` checks the venue it would leave: a position, a working order, the Bot on, an open bot or Nova trade, a Nova stock mode or approval, or an armed desk is at stake, and so is a check that cannot be read. Loading another Sim window starts the Sim's scratch account over, so its positions and working orders are at stake too. Then it refuses (`AGENT_AT_STAKE`) with each item, and the agent asks the operator; only after the operator's OK in the chat does it ask again with `confirm`. A venue change still disarms and still cancels Nova's working entries on the venue left (ADR 042) -- the refusal names that.
6. **Float is proven or unknown.** A float limit passes on the float Nova knew that day (the desk's enrichment snapshots, from 2026-07-28), or on SEC shares outstanding at or under the limit (a float cannot be larger): the newest count filed by the session and reported as of no more than 120 days before it, through the splits listed since. Shares issued after the count are not in it, so the answer carries the count's date. Anything else is "float unknown": counted and listed apart when asked, never dropped silently and never guessed from today's float.
7. **Voice and Jev are parked.** TypeSafe AI's Jev (a hosted decisions-only model that picks one of a list of options in about 0.3 s) fits a later command box or voice inside Nova -- matching words to a dictionary entry is a pick-from-a-list job -- but adds nothing while the operator types to an agent. It is a deferred issue, not built.

## Consequences

- "Find a small cap whose high was 300% with a float under 10M, show me the second one" is two agent calls; the desk lands on the day, paused before the run.
- The index takes hours to build on the first run (E: is shared with the downloader) and is only as current as its last build; `GET /api/agent` says which sessions it holds and how far it trails the files.
- Splits before June 2021 are not in Massive's list, so older suspects can only be flagged. Float on old days is mostly unknown, more so for delisted names.
- The Sim still plays at one speed; the first open of a day still waits for the import (seconds to minutes).
- Next: the strategy finder -- the bot strategies' detectors run over the movers' days, so "find a bull flag on a small cap that ran 100%" lists the setups and `show` opens the trigger.
