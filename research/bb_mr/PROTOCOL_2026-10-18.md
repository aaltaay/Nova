## 1. Research Questions & Hypotheses

1. **Primary Question (H1):** Does buying completed lower Bollinger Band rejections (`Low[t] <= Lower[t]` and `Close[t] > Lower[t]`) and exiting when executable bid reaches the dynamically updated completed upper Bollinger Band (while net basket liquidation P&L > 0) produce a repeatable positive net edge on U.S. stocks after realistic IBKR commissions, regulatory fees, bid-ask spread, slippage, and liquidity constraints?
2. **Grid / Additions Question (H2):** Does adding up to 5 entries per basket (spaced by at least $S$ completed signal candles) improve risk-adjusted return (Sharpe, Calmar, expectancy per dollar of exposure) compared to a single-entry baseline (`max_entries = 1`), or does it merely accumulate tail risk and mask losing entries until a basket stop-out?
3. **Universe Question (H3):** How does behavior differ between (A) a point-in-time Liquid-Stock Reference Universe ($5--$200, 20-day ADV >= $20M) and (B) a causal Morning Active-Stock Universe ($1--$30, >= +10% vs prior close, >= $500k cumulative dollar volume since 04:00 ET)?
4. **Session Question (H4):** How does the strategy perform across distinct intraday windows (`06:00-11:00`, `06:00-09:30`, `09:30-10:30`, `09:30-16:00` ET) and on a 1-hour (`H1`) timeframe (both intraday-flat and multi-day swing with overnight gaps and corporate actions)?

## 3. Point-in-Time Stock Universes (No Hindsight)

### 3.1 Universe A: Liquid-Stock Reference Universe (`liquid_reference`)
- **Eligibility at trading day $d$** (computed strictly from completed sessions $d-20 \dots d-1$):
  - U.S.-listed common stock (`type IN ('CS', 'ADRC')` or historical delisted 1--4 letter A--Z common stock with unpopulated `type` in Massive reference; ETFs, ETNs, ETVs, warrants, preferred shares, rights, units, funds, and OTC securities are excluded).
  - Listed exchange: `XNAS`, `XNYS`, `XASE`, `ARCX`, `BATS` (or null on historical delisted 1--4 letter common stocks; no OTC).
  - Prior regular-session close in day $d$'s split basis: $\$5.00 \le \text{prev\_close} \le \$200.00$, and current price at entry evaluation within $[\$5.00, \$200.00]$.
  - Prior 20 completed trading days' mean daily dollar volume: $\text{ADV}_{20}(d-1) \ge \$20,000,000$ with at least 20 prior trading days present.
  - Historical delisted common stocks are included whenever they meet the point-in-time criteria on day $d$.
  - Deterministic simultaneous-signal priority: ranked by point-in-time $\text{ADV}_{20}(d-1)$ descending, then symbol A--Z.

### 3.2 Universe B: Morning Active-Stock Universe (`morning_active`)
- **Causal minute-by-minute scanner at completed minute $t$**:
  - U.S. common stock (`CS`, `ADRC`, or 1--4 letter A--Z common ticker; excluding ETFs, warrants, preferreds, units, rights, OTC, and unrecorded likely splits).
  - Latest completed 1-minute close $P_t \in [\$1.00, \$30.00]$.
  - Gain vs. prior regular-session official close (causal split-adjusted to day $d$): $P_t \ge 1.10 \times \text{prev\_close}$.
  - Cumulative eligible dollar volume from `04:00` a.m. ET through completed minute $t$: $\text{CumDollarVol}(t) = \sum_{04:00 < s \le t} \text{close}_s \times \text{volume}_s \ge \$500,000$.
  - Ranked at minute $t$ by $\text{CumDollarVol}(t)$ descending (ties broken by symbol A--Z); **top 20** selected at each completed minute $t$.
  - If a stock drops out of the top 20 at a later minute while a basket is already open, that basket remains managed (risk monitoring, normal exits, and additions if eligible). Scanner removal is not an automatic exit.
  - Labeled explicitly as an **active low-priced-stock universe ($1--$30)** because point-in-time float is only partially covered historically (`sec_shares` from SEC filings).

## 4. Signal, Indicator, and Bar Construction

- **Standard Deviation Convention:** Population standard deviation (`ddof = 0`) over the last $N$ completed signal candle closes:
  $$\text{Middle}[t] = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=0}^{N-1} C_{t-i}, \quad \sigma[t] = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=0}^{N-1}(C_{t-i} - \text{Middle}[t])^2}$$
  $$\text{Upper}[t] = \text{Middle}[t] + K\sigma[t], \quad \text{Lower}[t] = \text{Middle}[t] - K\sigma[t]$$
- **No Forward-Filling:** Zero-volume minutes with no prints do not emit fictitious bars. Multi-minute signal bars (`5m`, `1h`) aggregate observed 1-minute bars within each clock-aligned bucket (`[09:30, 09:35)`, etc.) and emit a completed candle at the bucket end only when at least one trade printed in that bucket.
- **Warmup Across Sessions:** Preceding sessions' bars (causal split-adjusted to day $d$'s price basis using only splits with $\text{execution\_date} \le d$) warm up the $N$-bar rolling window. No signal is emitted until at least $N$ valid completed signal bars exist.
- **Long Entry Signal on Completed Signal Candle $t$:**
  - $\text{Low}[t] \le \text{Lower}[t]$ and $\text{Close}[t] > \text{Lower}[t]$.
  - Evaluated **once** when signal candle $t$ completes. Never re-triggered on subsequent minutes of the same signal candle.
  - Additions require a **new** qualifying signal candle $t'$ with $\text{bar\_index}(t') - \text{bar\_index}(t_{\text{last\_fill}}) \ge S$ (counting observed completed signal candles of that stock).

## 5. Portfolio, Basket, Sizing, and Risk Rules

- **Starting Equity:** $\$25,000.00$, shared across all stocks in a run.
- **Basket Reference Equity ($E_0$):** Frozen to current account equity when a basket's first entry is initiated.
- **Sizing & Exposure Caps:**
  - Per-entry target allocation: $2.0\% \times E_0 \times \text{multiplier}^{k-1}$ ($k \in \{1 \dots \text{max\_entries}\}$).
  - Max entries per basket: $5$ (or $1, 3, 5$ in grid).
  - Max committed entry capital per basket: $10.0\% \times E_0$.
  - Max simultaneously held stocks: $5$.
  - Max aggregate gross exposure for new purchases: $50.0\%$ of current account equity.
  - No margin borrowing or leverage.
  - Whole shares only ($\text{shares} = \lfloor (\text{alloc} - \text{est\_fee}) / \text{exec\_ask} \rfloor \ge 1$).
  - Pending orders reserve cash immediately so simultaneous signals cannot double-spend cash.
- **Normal Basket Exit (1-minute scheduled evaluation):**
  - Liquidate basket when $\text{executable\_bid} \ge \text{Upper}[t_{\text{latest\_completed}}]$ AND estimated net basket liquidation P&L (after actual opening fees and estimated closing fees + exit slippage) $> 0$.
- **Risk Exits (every quote/bar replay event):**
  - **Basket Loss Limit:** When basket net liquidation P&L $\le -0.5\% \times E_0$, submit immediate liquidation order (`basket_stop`).
  - **Account Daily Loss Limit:** When account daily P&L (`equity - day_start_equity`) $\le -3.0\% \times \text{day\_start\_equity}$, cancel pending entries, liquidate all open baskets (`daily_loss_stop`), and block new entries until the next trading day.
  - Risk exits trigger orders filled at next executable prices (never guaranteed to stop at the threshold during gaps or halts).

## 6. Execution Model, Fees, and Extended-Hours Policy

- **Fee Schedule:** Exact IBKR Pro Fixed + regulatory pass-throughs (`backend/practice/fees.py`):
  - Commission: $\min(\max(\$0.005 \times \text{shares}, \$1.00), 0.01 \times \text{trade\_value})$.
  - SEC Section 31 fee on sells ($0.0000206 \times \text{proceeds}$).
  - FINRA TAF on sells ($\$0.000195/\text{share}$, max $\$9.79$, $0$ during Q4 2026 holiday).
  - FINRA CAT fee on buys and sells ($\$0.000003/\text{share}$).
- **Execution Timing & Prices:**
  - Never fill retrospectively at the signal candle's close. Orders submitted after signal candle $t$ closes execute at the earliest eligible subsequent event ($t+1$).
  - **Tier 1 (Bar-quote proxy for full 5-year runs & 96-grid):** Next-bar `open[t+1]` $\pm$ modeled half-spread $h = \max(\$0.01, \alpha_{\text{session}} \times (\text{high}_{t+1} - \text{low}_{t+1}))$ ($\alpha = 0.15$ premarket, $0.08$ regular hours) $\pm$ adverse slippage ($\$0.01/\text{share}$ base).
  - **Tier 2 (Tick-level NBBO `quotes_v1` + `trades_v1` validation):** Direct execution against Massive nanosecond SIP NBBO bid/ask quotes after $250\text{ ms}$ latency, rejecting stale ($>15\text{ s}$), locked, crossed, or wide ($>5\%$) quotes, and applying size-dependent slippage when order shares exceed displayed top-of-book size.
- **Extended-Hours Marketable-Limit Policy:** Outside `09:30-16:00` ET, entries use marketable limit orders with a $+1.0\%$ price collar above the signal-close reference price and `60s` TIF expiration.
- **Liquidity & Participation Cap:** Order size at submission is capped at $10\%$ of trailing observed 5-bar mean minute volume; fill size per minute bar is capped at $10\%$ of that bar's volume (excess shares partially fill and carry forward to subsequent bars).
- **Halts & Cutoff Behavior:** Zero-volume / missing minutes do not fill orders. If a position cannot liquidate at window cutoff due to a halt or missing prints, it is retained with real exposure until the next tradable print/quote (even across session boundaries).

## 7. Chronological Splits & Walk-Forward Design

Across the 5-year store (`2021-10-19` to `2026-09-21`, 1,235 trading days after 20-day daily ADV warmup from `2021-09-21`):

1. **Development (Train) Period:** `2021-10-19` to `2024-06-28` (676 trading days).
2. **Validation (Selection) Period:** `2024-07-01` to `2025-06-30` (250 trading days).
3. **Final Evaluation (Out-of-Sample Test) Period:** `2025-07-01` to `2026-09-21` (309 trading days).
4. **Rolling Walk-Forward:** 6 rolling folds (250-trading-day train window $\to$ 125-trading-day out-of-sample test window, stepped by 125 trading days). At every fold boundary, any open basket is forcibly liquidated at the boundary's last executable bid before scoring that fold so open-basket outcomes never leak across folds.

## 8. Pre-Registered Baselines, 96-Grid Search, Selection Rule, and Verdict Criteria

### 8.1 Fixed Baselines (Run First)
- **Baseline A:** `5m`, $N=100$, $K=2.0$, $S=10$, $\text{max\_entries}=5$, $\text{mult}=1.0$.
- **Baseline B:** `5m`, $N=100$, $K=2.0$, $S=10$, $\text{max\_entries}=1$, $\text{mult}=1.0$ (plus exposure-matched variant `B_exp_matched` where single-entry allocation is scaled to match Baseline A's average capital deployed).
- **Baseline C:** `5m`, $N=200$, $K=2.5$, $S=10$, $\text{max\_entries}=5$, $\text{mult}=1.0$.
- **Baseline D:** `1h` (`H1`), $N=100$, $K=2.0$, $S=10$, $\text{max\_entries}=5$, $\text{mult}=1.0$ (tested both as `D1_intraday_flat` and `D2_swing_overnight`).

### 8.2 Bounded Intraday Grid (96 Configurations per Universe on Primary Morning Window `06:00-11:00` ET)
- `timeframe`: `["1m", "5m"]` (2)
- `period`: `[20, 50, 100, 200]` (4)
- `deviation`: `[2.0, 2.5]` (2)
- `spacing`: `[5, 10]` (2)
- `max_entries`: `[1, 3, 5]` (3)
- `multiplier`: `[1.0]` (1)
- Total: $2 \times 4 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 1 = 96$ configurations per universe.

### 8.3 Frozen Candidate Selection Rule (Pre-Registered)
To select a candidate from Development (`2021-10-19`..`2024-06-28`) + Validation (`2024-07-01`..`2025-06-30`) before touching the Final Evaluation set (`2025-07-01`..`2026-09-21`):
1. Require at least $100$ baskets in Development and $40$ baskets in Validation.
2. Require positive net dollar P&L ($\text{PF} > 1.05$) in **both** Development and Validation.
3. Require that >50% of immediate parameter neighbors also have $\text{PF} > 1.0$.
4. Among surviving configurations, rank by Validation net Calmar / Sharpe ratio (`mean_daily_return / std_daily_return * sqrt(252)`). If zero configurations pass steps 1--3, the top configuration by Validation Sharpe is still evaluated on Test for diagnostic completeness, and the selection gate failure is reported.

### 8.4 Pre-Registered Promotion / Verdict Criteria
A strategy configuration earns **`promising for paper-forward testing`** if and only if on the untouched Final Evaluation period (`2025-07-01`..`2026-09-21`) and full 5-year walk-forward:
1. Net profit factor $\ge 1.20$ after full IBKR commissions, fees, spread, and slippage.
2. Positive net P&L under $2\times$ transaction costs/slippage stress ($\text{PF} > 1.00$).
3. Maximum marked-to-market drawdown $< 20\%$.
4. Top-5 winning days and top-5 winning stocks account for $< 80\%$ of total net profit (not dependent on a handful of outlier trades).
5. Block-bootstrap 95% confidence interval lower bound on annualized Sharpe $> 0$.

If positive in some subsets/periods but failing robustness or sample size, verdict is **`inconclusive`**. If net negative or destroyed by realistic costs/stress tests, verdict is **`no credible edge`**.
